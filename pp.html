Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Exhalo. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage for our content creation app designed for Pinterest creators.

Exhalo is operated by Exhalo. If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@exhalo.app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services to you. The types of information we collect include:

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account or use our services, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials
  • Profile information you choose to provide

2.2 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with our services:

  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • IP address and general location data
  • Pages visited and features used
  • Time and date of access
  • Error logs and diagnostic data

2.3 Third-Party Integration Data

When you connect third-party services like Pinterest to your Exhalo account, we access certain data from these platforms as described in Section 3 below.

3. Pinterest API Data Usage

Important Information About Pinterest Data: We access your Pinterest data through the official Pinterest API to provide our services. This section explains exactly how we handle your Pinterest information.

3.1 What Pinterest Data We Access

When you authorize Exhalo to connect with your Pinterest account, we request access to:

  • Your Pinterest boards and their details
  • Your pins (including images, descriptions, and metadata)
  • Your public profile information
  • Permission to create and schedule new pins on your behalf

3.2 Real-Time Access Only – No Data Storage

We do NOT store Pinterest API data on our servers. All Pinterest data you see in Exhalo is accessed in real-time directly from Pinterest's servers when you use our app. This means:

  • We fetch your boards and pins on-demand when you need them
  • We do not maintain copies or backups of your Pinterest content
  • When you disconnect Pinterest, we immediately lose access to your data
  • Your Pinterest data remains solely on Pinterest's servers

3.3 How We Use Pinterest Data

We use your Pinterest data exclusively to provide the following services to you:

  • Pin Search & Inspiration: Display your existing pins to help you find inspiration for new content
  • Pin Creation: Create new pins on your boards with your explicit instruction
  • Pin Scheduling: Schedule pins to be published at times you specify
  • Board Management: Show your boards so you can select where to publish content

We use Pinterest data only to provide these services directly to you. We do not use your Pinterest data for any other purpose, including analytics, advertising, or training machine learning models.

3.4 No Third-Party Sharing of Pinterest Data

We never share, sell, rent, or trade your Pinterest data with third parties. Your Pinterest information is accessed solely to provide services to you and is not disclosed to anyone else.

3.5 Your Control Over Pinterest Data

You have complete control over Exhalo's access to your Pinterest data:

  • Disconnect Anytime: You can revoke Exhalo's access to your Pinterest account at any time through your Pinterest account settings or through Exhalo's app settings
  • Selective Authorization: You control what permissions you grant to Exhalo during the Pinterest authorization process
  • Immediate Effect: When you disconnect, Exhalo immediately loses all access to your Pinterest data

3.6 Compliance with Pinterest Policies

Exhalo fully complies with:

  • Pinterest API Terms of Service
  • Pinterest Developer Policy
  • Pinterest Community Guidelines

We are committed to using Pinterest's API responsibly and in accordance with all applicable policies and guidelines.

5. Data Retention & Security

5.1 Data Retention

Pinterest Data: As stated in Section 3.2, we do not store Pinterest API data. We access it in real-time only when needed.

Account Data: We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting support@exhalo.app.

Usage Data: We retain usage logs and analytics data for up to 90 days for troubleshooting and service improvement purposes.

5.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Access controls limiting employee access to personal data
  • Secure storage practices for any data we do retain

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data using reasonable measures.

5.3 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you promptly in accordance with applicable laws and take appropriate steps to mitigate any harm.

9. International Users & GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

9.1 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: You have given explicit consent for specific processing activities (e.g., connecting Pinterest)
  • Contract Performance: Processing is necessary to provide services you've requested
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests (e.g., improving our service, security) that do not override your rights
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is required by law

9.2 GDPR Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Obtain confirmation of what personal data we process and receive a copy
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing)
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

9.3 Data Transfers

Exhalo is based in the United States. If you are located outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers in compliance with GDPR requirements.

9.4 Contact for GDPR Inquiries

For any GDPR-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at support@exhalo.app with "GDPR Request" in the subject line.

10.3 Right to Opt-Out of Sale
